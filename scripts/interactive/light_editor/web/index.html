Shortcuts

        
      

      

        

          
View

          

            LMB drag orbit · RMB drag pan · wheel zoom ·
            F frame the selection

            WASD walk the view ·
            Space/E up · Q down ·
            Shift faster · Alt finer — with
            nothing selected; with a selection the same keys nudge it

            C hide camera bodies · V camera preview windows ·
            G composition guides · ShiftG ground grid

            top-left Exterior left/right stand you where that sensor stands and
            leave the scene editable — Enter camera, on the Cameras tab, is the
            one that hands the sensor your keys
          

        

        

          
Selection

          

            click an object or its row to select it · Shift+click adds ·
            CtrlA selects every object · Esc deselects
          

        

        

          
Moving what's selected

          

            drag the gizmo to move, turn or scale it ·
            M move gizmo · R rotate gizmo

            the mode bar itself drags anywhere in the viewport ·
            double-click its background to put it back at the top

            WASD/Space/Ctrl nudges along X/Y/Z ·
            +/− scales ·
            hold Shift for 5 cm steps, Alt for 1 mm

            CtrlD duplicates the selection ·
            Del removes it (undoable) ·
            CtrlZ undo · CtrlShiftZ redo
          

        

        

          
Flying a camera

          

            select a camera to look through it ·
            WASD walk · Space up · Ctrl down ·
            drag to aim

            hold Shift to move faster, Alt for finer control ·
            wheel sets speed · Esc exits
